## Communities and Collaboration

I collaborate with scientific and professional communities that connect environmental research, hydrology, citizen science, and open methods for monitoring rivers and water resources.

### European Citizen Science Association (ECSA)

**Membership**

Member of ECSA, supporting participatory approaches to environmental research and community engagement.

### International Association of Hydrological Sciences (IAHS)

**Membership**

Member of IAHS, contributing to an international community focused on hydrological science and water research.

### IAHS MOXXI Working Group

**Working group involvement**

Member of the IAHS Measurements and Observations in the XXI Century (MOXXI) Working Group, which brings together researchers developing innovative approaches to hydrological monitoring and observations.
